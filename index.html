How to listen

You are a small light in a dead sea. You cannot see the world. You can only hear it.

  • A / D or ← → — drift
  • W / Space — rise / leap
  • S — descend / hold stillness
  • J or Left Click — pulse (reveals a wavefront)
  • K — bloom (hold to sing a sustained tone)
  • L — stillness (briefly unheard)
  • Esc — pause

Every pulse attracts. Every silence forgets. Listen for the key of each place and move in harmony with it.
